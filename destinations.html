Popular Destinations

Explore our handpicked selection of amazing places to visit

Paris, France

Best Time to Visit: April to June, September to November

Famous For: Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, Notre-Dame Cathedral

Paris is the capital and most populous city of France. Known as the "City of Light," Paris is home to some of the world's most famous monuments and attractions. Walk along the Champs-Élysées, visit the iconic Eiffel Tower, explore the artistic treasures at the Louvre, and savor authentic French cuisine at charming bistros.

Top Attractions:

  • Eiffel Tower
  • Louvre Museum
  • Arc de Triomphe
  • Notre-Dame Cathedral
  • Sacré-Cœur Basilica

Shanghai, China

Best Time to Visit: September to November

Famous For: Iconic Architecture, Skyline buildings, Shopping & Dining

Shanghai is a global financial powerhouse and China's largest city, renowned for its futuristic Lujiazui skyline—including the towering Shanghai Tower—and the historic colonial-era architecture along The Bund

Top Attractions:

  • Shanghai Tower
  • Nanjinglu Street
  • Longhua Temple
  • Jin Mao Tower
  • Tianzifang

Tokyo, Japan

Best Time to Visit: March to May, September to November

Famous For: Technology, Temples, Cherry Blossoms, Cuisine

Tokyo is a vibrant city that seamlessly blends ancient traditions with cutting-edge technology. Experience the serenity of traditional temples, the excitement of busy shopping districts, delicious Japanese cuisine, and incredible technological innovations. From historic shrines to modern skyscrapers, Tokyo offers endless discoveries.

Top Attractions:

  • Senso-ji Temple
  • Tokyo Skytree
  • Shibuya Crossing
  • Meiji Shrine
  • Tsukiji Fish Market

Bali, Indonesia

Best Time to Visit: April to October

Famous For: Beaches, Rice Terraces, Temples, Surfing

Bali is an Indonesian island paradise known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beautiful beaches, and coral reefs. Experience the island's unique Hindu culture, visit ancient temples, enjoy world-class surfing, and relax in luxurious resorts surrounded by natural beauty.

Top Attractions:

  • Tanah Lot Temple
  • Tegallalang Rice Terraces
  • Ubud Monkey Forest
  • Mount Batur
  • Seminyak Beach
